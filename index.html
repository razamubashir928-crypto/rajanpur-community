WHY WE STARTED

Every community deserves to be heard.

Rajanpur Community Initiative is an independent, community-led effort to collect residents' opinions, understand local concerns and explore practical solutions.

Our aim is to organize feedback responsibly and share clear findings that may support constructive discussions with relevant organizations and public departments.

We do not represent a government department. Participation does not guarantee official action. Findings will be described according to the responses actually collected.
